Fluent-ish is a vocabulary trainer that listens to you say a word and grades
your pronunciation. This policy explains what happens to that audio and to
everything else the app touches.

## What we collect

Nothing. Fluent-ish has no account system, no sign-in, no analytics, no
advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect, store, or
transmit your name, email address, contacts, location, or device
identifiers. We cannot identify you, because we never receive anything that
could.

## Your speech

To grade a word, the app records a short snippet of audio while you are
speaking and converts it to text. How that happens depends on the language:

- **Latin** is transcribed entirely on your device, using a speech model
  that the app downloads once and then runs locally. This audio never
  leaves your iPhone.
- **Spanish, French, German and Japanese** use Apple's speech recognition.
  Where iOS supports on-device recognition for a language, the app requests
  it, and the audio stays on your device. Where iOS does not, the audio is
  sent to Apple for transcription. In that case Apple's handling of it is
  governed by Apple's own privacy policy, at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

In every case the recording is used only to produce a transcript, is held
only for the moment it takes to do that, and is never written to disk,
retained, or sent to us. We operate no servers and receive no audio.

## What stays on your device

Your study progress -- which words you have seen, how you scored, and when
each word is next due -- is stored locally on your iPhone. It is not synced,
backed up to us, or shared. Deleting the app deletes it.

If you study Latin, the downloaded speech model is also stored locally.
Deleting the app deletes that too.

## Network use

The app makes one kind of network request: downloading the Latin speech
model from Hugging Face, over HTTPS, and only after you explicitly confirm
a prompt that names the download size. If you never choose Latin, the app
makes no network requests of its own.

Speech sent to Apple for transcription, as described above, is the only
other case where data leaves your device.

## Children

Fluent-ish does not collect personal information from anyone, including
children under 13.

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with an
